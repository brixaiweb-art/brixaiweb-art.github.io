Personal Video Uploader

This is a private, single-user tool. It uploads videos that the owner creates to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Privacy policy

The app uses Google sign-in only to get permission to upload videos to, and manage videos on, the owner's own YouTube channel (the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl scopes).

  • It collects no personal data from anyone else.
  • The OAuth token stays on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • Google user data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos. It is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
